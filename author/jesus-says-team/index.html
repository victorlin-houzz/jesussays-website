Faith Library authors

The Jesus Says team

The Jesus Says team makes Jesus Says: Daily Reflection, an iPhone and iPad app for a daily practice with Scripture, and writes the 37 guides in the Faith Library. Every guide on this site is credited to the team.

Who reviews the guides

Victor reads and approves each guide before it is published or updated.

How the guides are written

  1. One guide for each real question. Each guide answers a question people bring to Scripture, such as how to pray when they can’t sleep or whether anger is a sin. When a guide already answers a question, we improve that guide instead of adding a near-copy.

  2. Drafted with AI writing tools. Guides are drafted and revised with the help of AI writing tools, working from our written editorial guidelines.

  3. Scripture checked word for word. Before a guide can be published, a script checks every quotation against the King James text bundled in the app. Verses are read in context, and a guide says so when a popular verse is often misapplied.

  4. Read and approved by a person. Nothing in the Faith Library is published automatically. A person on the team reads each new guide or update and approves it before it goes live.

  5. Care notes on hard topics. Guides on depression, grief, anxiety, addiction, illness, and relationships point readers to pastors, counselors, doctors, and crisis lines such as 988.

The full rules are in our editorial standards.
