# Faith Library editorial guidelines

The Faith Library is small on purpose. In May–June 2026 an automated pipeline grew it from ~28 reviewed guides to
~520 pages, ~470 of them keyword-swapped template clones. That pattern reads as spam to people and to search
engines (Google's scaled-content-abuse policy), so the library was consolidated to a few dozen careful guides in
September 2026 and every new or updated article must meet these standards. `scripts/generate_article.py` sends this
file to the model as its instructions, and `scripts/check_aeo.py` enforces the checkable parts.

## Article body format
The site chrome (head, nav, eyebrow tag, H1, byline, app CTA, related links, footer, JSON-LD) is generated by
`scripts/build_site.py` from `_data/library.json`. An article file only owns the body between the
`<!-- article:body -->` markers.

**There is no fixed article template.** A library where every piece has the same number of sections, the same
heading shapes and the same closing block reads as unedited generation, to readers and to search engines, and it
is judged across the whole site. Let the question decide the shape: a prayer page can be mostly the prayer and a
few notes on praying it; a question like "Is anger a sin?" may need two sections, a comparison may need a table.

Fixed requirements (checked by `scripts/check_aeo.py`):
   - First element: a `<p>` direct answer of 40–60 words that answers the searcher's question in its first
     sentence ("Pray the way Jesus taught in Matthew 6:9–13: …", not "Many people wonder…"). Context and caveats
     come after the answer. No preamble like "In this article".
   - No paragraph longer than 6 sentences: one idea per paragraph. Split long prayers into short stanzas (several
     `<p>` inside the one `<div class="prayer">`); put enumerations in a list and comparisons in a table.
   - At least 2 links to distinct, independent primary sources, placed inline where the text relies on them:
     the passage in context (e.g. Bible Gateway, KJV), a verse with its Hebrew/Greek (Blue Letter Bible) when you
     explain a word, the official site of a crisis line named in a care note, a competitor's own site for a
     comparison. Link the source itself, not a blog summarising it, and check the link resolves. Word each link
     for its article; never paste a stock "Sources" block or sentence.
   - Paragraphs stand on their own: don't open a paragraph with "This", "It", "They", or "Such"; name the verse,
     the prayer, or the person instead, so the paragraph still makes sense when quoted by itself.

Building blocks (use what the piece needs, in the order it needs):
   - `<aside class="care-note"><p><strong>Care note:</strong> …</p></aside>` right after the direct answer for
     sensitive topics (see below).
   - `<h2>` sections, as many as the question needs. Vary heading shapes: some can be questions people ask, most
     should be plain statements of what the section gives ("Three verses for the middle of the night"). Use
     `<h3>`, `<p>`, `<ol>`, `<ul>`, `<strong>`, `<em>`, `<a>`, `<blockquote class="verse">` and tables
     (`<div class="table-wrap"><table>…</table></div>`). No inline styles, no classes other than those listed here.
   - Verse lists: `<li><strong>Psalm 34:18</strong> — “KJV text.” One or two sentences of application.</li>`
   - Standalone verse: `<blockquote class="verse"><p>KJV text</p><cite>Psalm 46:10</cite></blockquote>`
   - A written prayer, if the page has one: `<div class="prayer"><p>…</p></div>` (one per page max).
   - A real app screenshot, only on comparison pages or where the one allowed app sentence needs it:
     `<figure class="art-shot"><img src="/assets/screens/2.0/…webp" alt="what the screen shows" width="660"
     height="1434" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>…</figcaption></figure>`.
   - Optional FAQ, only when readers have real follow-up questions the body doesn't answer: must be the last
     element, `<section class="faq"><h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>` then 3–7 pairs of
     `<h3>Question?</h3><p>Answer</p>` then `</section>`. Answers 35–80 words, self-contained (they are exported
     as FAQPage JSON-LD), and must not repeat the body. Leave it out rather than invent questions.
   - Total body length roughly 800–1,400 words. Tight and useful beats long.

Catalog entry fields in `_data/library.json`: `title` (SEO title without the " — Jesus Says" suffix, ≤ 60 chars),
`h1`, `description` (140–160 chars, plain, no hype), `label` (≤ 40 chars, library link text), `category`, `kind`,
`published`, `modified`, and three `related` slugs.

## Scripture rules (most important)
- The app's reader is the King James Version, so the site quotes KJV. Get exact text with:
  `python3 scripts/kjv.py "Philippians 4:6-7" "1 Peter 5:7"` (reads the app's bundled Bible from ../quotebible)
  Never quote from memory; never paraphrase inside quotation marks. `scripts/check_aeo.py` rejects any quote that
  is not verbatim KJV.
- Adjust commentary so it matches the KJV wording you quote (e.g. KJV 1 Peter 5:7 is "Casting all your care upon
  him" — don't then discuss the word "anxiety" as if it were in the quote). You may explain an archaic word
  briefly ("careful" in Phil 4:6 means anxious).
- Every verse must fit its context. No proof-texting (Jeremiah 29:11 is to exiles; Psalm 91 is not a guarantee
  against all harm; healing verses are not promises of cure). Say so gently where it matters.
- 4–7 verses per page is plenty. Choose the best, not the most.

## App mentions and positioning
- Do NOT promote the app in the body. The chrome adds one standard App Store call-to-action. At most one short,
  natural sentence in the body may mention a real app feature if it truly helps the reader. Remove every
  existing "Jesus Says sends you personalized…", "confession journal", "Pray Out Loud — with a Voice That Guides
  You" style pitch, and any FAQ answer that plugs the app.
- Real app features (only these, in this language): "Today with God" daily card (one verse, one reflection,
  one prayer, one step); speak or type a private reflection and receive Scripture, a short reflection, and a
  prayer; a 7-day path through Scripture; Be Still quiet moments (Still Waters, Peace Be Still, Consider the
  Heavens); Listen Mode read-aloud; private Verse Library; private journal with calendar view; full KJV Bible.
  iPhone and iPad. Free download with 7 days of full access, then Plus subscription.
- Never: "AI", "AI-powered", "AI Bible", "chat", "companion that talks back", "Jesus speaks to you through the
  app", manifesting/universe/energy, or describing the app with "therapy", "mental health", "wellness",
  "mood tracker". (Articles MAY and SHOULD point readers to counselors, doctors, pastors, and 988 — that is care
  guidance, not app positioning.)
- Never claim reflections stay on the device or that nothing is shared.

## Care and safety
- Sensitive topics (depression, hopelessness, anxiety, grief/loss, loneliness, addiction, illness/healing,
  money crisis, job loss, anger, relationships/marriage, forgiveness) need a care note: prayer and Scripture
  support care but do not replace pastoral, medical, counseling, or emergency help; in the US call or text 988
  (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline); outside the US contact local emergency services.
- Relationships, marriage, forgiveness, anger: include an abuse boundary — forgiveness and reconciliation never
  require staying in danger; contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-7233, US) or local help.
- Addiction: recovery support (pastor, recovery group, doctor; SAMHSA helpline 1-800-662-4357 in the US).
- No medical, financial, or legal claims. No invented statistics or studies. No "research shows".

## Voice
- Warm, plain, pastoral, specific. Write like a thoughtful pastor who respects the reader's time.
- Cut filler and AI tells: "journey" overuse, "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "not X but Y" tics
  in every paragraph, "powerful", "transformative", triple-adjective lists, rhetorical questions stacked up,
  em-dash in every sentence. Vary sentence length. Contractions are fine.
- Non-denominational and respectful of Protestant and Catholic readers.
- American English. Curly quotes “ ” ’ are fine.

## Internal links
Add 1–3 contextual links to other live articles (`/content/<slug>.html`, slugs from `_data/library.json`). Never link
to a redirected or removed URL; the build fails if you do. You may link to `/play/still-waters/` once when a
quiet-minute pause fits the topic (anxiety, peace, sleep, stress, grief).

## One page per question
Before writing, check `_data/library.json`. If an existing article already answers the searcher's question, improve
that article instead of adding a near-duplicate; `check_aeo.py` fails pages that share too many phrases. Never
generate a page by swapping a keyword into a template ("What would Jesus say about Acts").

## Final self-check
- `python3 scripts/build_site.py && python3 scripts/check_aeo.py` both pass.
- `python3 scripts/kjv.py` used for every quote; every quote is verbatim KJV.
- First element is the 40–60 word direct-answer `<p>`; no paragraph over 6 sentences; at least 2 primary-source links.
- The piece's shape fits its question, not the last article's: section count, heading shapes and closing vary; an FAQ only if it earns its place (last, 3–7 pairs).
- No app pitch in body/FAQ beyond one optional sentence; no banned words.
- Care note present when required.
- HTML fragment is well-formed (balanced tags). Links only to allowed slugs.
