About Jesus Says

Jesus Says is a Christian Scripture, prayer, and devotional companion built to help people find Bible-based encouragement for real-life moments.

What We Publish

The Jesus Says website publishes Bible verse guides, prayer resources, confession guides, daily devotionals, and practical Christian advice. Each article is written to answer a specific spiritual question clearly, then point readers back to Scripture, prayer, wise community, and care when needed.

About the App

The Jesus Says iPhone app helps people receive personalized Bible verses, voice prayer prompts, confession journaling support, and daily devotional reflection. It is a companion for personal faith practice, not a replacement for church, pastoral care, counseling, medical care, or emergency help.
