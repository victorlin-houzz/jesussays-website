Jesus Says vs Glorify: Which Devotional App Fits You?

By the Jesus Says teamAI-assisted, reviewed by VictorUpdated September 29, 2026

Choose Glorify for a guided daily routine: a quote, a Bible passage, a devotional, a guided meditation, and worship music, plus friends and church groups. Choose Jesus Says for a short Scripture reflection that starts with your day: speak or type what you’re carrying and receive Scripture, a short reflection, and a prayer. Both rely on paid subscriptions.

What does Glorify do best?

Glorify is designed to make a daily quiet time easy to keep. Each session follows the same order: an encouraging quote, a Bible passage, a devotional that connects the two, and a guided meditation called the Daily Walk with God. Glorify’s App Store page says the whole thing fits into about ten minutes, and its website says the daily devotionals are written by its theologian team.

Music is a big part of the app. There’s a song of the day and a larger collection of worship music, along with sleep stories, calming Scripture-based meditations, and multi-day audio courses that Glorify’s site says are led by authors and theologians. If you worship best with a song playing, that alone may decide it.

Glorify is also more social than Jesus Says. You can connect with friends, family, and church groups, talk through the day’s devotional, share prayer requests, and pray for others. It offers several Bible translations, including the King James Version, and is available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese on iPhone and Android.

What does Jesus Says do differently?

Jesus Says is a daily Scripture reflection and prayer app. It has a daily card of its own, Today with God, with one verse, one reflection, one prayer, and one step. The larger difference is that you can bring your own day to it: speak or type what you’re carrying and receive Scripture, a short reflection, and a prayer you can return to later.

Alongside Today with God, Jesus Says offers a 7-day path through Scripture, Be Still quiet moments (Still Waters; Peace, Be Still; and Consider the Heavens) that each end with a verse, Listen Mode read-aloud, a private Verse Library, a private journal with a calendar view, and the full King James Bible. It runs on iPhone and iPad and is a free download with 7 days of full access, then a Plus subscription.

What it leaves out matters too. There’s no worship music, no narrated courses, no community, one Bible translation, and no Android app. Jesus Says generates its Scripture-based reflections; they are not reviewed by clergy, and they don’t replace your church or pastor.

Should you choose Jesus Says or Glorify?

  • Choose Glorify if you want the same guided shape every morning, if worship music helps you pray, or if you’d like to share the practice with friends or a small group.
  • Choose Jesus Says if you want a smaller daily practice, prefer quiet over music, and want to start from what’s actually on your heart that day.
  • Try both before paying for either. Both are free to download, and a week with each will tell you more than a feature list.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Glorify free?

Glorify is free to download, and its website invites you to try it for free. Ongoing access to the full app is through a paid Glorify Plus subscription, offered as monthly and annual plans. Prices vary by region and change from time to time, so check the App Store or Google Play listing before you subscribe.

Does Jesus Says have worship music?

No. Jesus Says has no music library. Its audio is Listen Mode, which reads your daily practice aloud, and Be Still offers quiet visual moments that each end with a verse. If singing or listening to worship is central to how you pray, Glorify is the better choice.

Who writes the devotionals in Glorify and Jesus Says?

Glorify says its daily devotionals are written by its theologian team, and its courses are led by authors and theologians. Jesus Says generates its Scripture-based reflections and prayers, and they are not reviewed by religious authorities. With any devotional app, read what you’re given alongside the Bible and bring questions to your pastor.

Is Glorify available on iPad or Android?

Glorify’s App Store listing marks it as an iPhone app, and it also has an Android version on Google Play. Jesus Says runs on iPhone and iPad but has no Android app. If you pray on an Android phone, Glorify is the option of the two that will work for you.

Can I use Glorify and Jesus Says together?

You can. Glorify could anchor a morning devotional with music, and Jesus Says could hold a short reflection and journal entry later in the day. Most people won’t want two subscriptions, though, so try both and keep the one that fits the time you really have.
