What Jesus Says Does

  1. Today with God — Scripture, a reflection, a prayer, and a practical step for today.
  2. Speak what you are carrying — Speak or type a private reflection and receive Scripture-based guidance.
  3. A 7-day path through Scripture — A new passage and prompt each day.
  4. Be Still — Touch Still Waters, calm the sea, or look up at a sky full of stars. Try Still Waters now.
  5. Listen and rest — Listen Mode reads your daily practice aloud on your device.
  6. Save what matters — A private Verse Library, journal, and the bundled King James Bible.
