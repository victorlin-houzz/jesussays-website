What the app does

  1. Today with God. Scripture, prayer and small steps, with a sparrow companion you can name and a Peace Garden that grows alongside your practice.

  2. Speak what you are carrying. Speak or type a private reflection and receive Scripture, a short reflection, and a prayer you can return to later.

  3. A 7-day path through Scripture. Choose a devotion path and receive a new passage and prompt each day.

  4. Walk with Elijah. Follow a seven-day painted retelling, choose or write story replies, and read the linked Bible passages. Stories are retellings; the passages carry the words of Scripture.

  5. Peace Garden. Earn seeds through practice, discover Scripture-rooted plants and choose seeds in the illustrated Seed Shed with previews of grown plants.

  6. Be Still. Touch still water and watch the ink settle, calm a storm-tossed sea (Mark 4:39), or look up at a sky full of stars (Psalm 147:4). Each ends with a verse. Try Still Waters in your browser.

  7. Listen and rest. Listen Mode reads your daily practice aloud with text-to-speech on your device.

  8. Save what matters. A private Verse Library, a journal with a calendar view, and the full King James Bible with every reference tappable.

Good to know

  • Available for iPhone and iPad. There is no Android version.

  • Reflections and prayers are generated from Scripture and are not reviewed by clergy. The app is a companion for prayer, not a replacement for church, pastoral care, counseling, or emergency help.

  • Help is available from Profile and the prayer/reflection help controls. Story threads include help in their About sheet.

  • The bundled KJV Bible and Be Still experiences work offline and remain free. Generated responses need an internet connection. Sign in with Apple is optional, including for purchases. Restore Purchases is available in Profile for non-premium users. Any eligible introductory offer, its duration, price and renewal terms appear on the purchase screen.
