What the app does

  1. Today with God. A simple daily card brings together Scripture, a reflection, a prayer, and a practical step for today.
  2. Speak what you are carrying. Speak or type a private reflection and receive Scripture, a short reflection, and a prayer you can return to later.
  3. A 7-day path through Scripture. Choose a devotion path and receive a new passage and prompt each day.
  4. Be Still. Touch still water and watch the ink settle, calm a storm-tossed sea (Mark 4:39), or look up at a sky full of stars (Psalm 147:4). Each ends with a verse. Try Still Waters in your browser.
  5. Listen and rest. Listen Mode reads your daily practice aloud with text-to-speech on your device.
  6. Save what matters. A private Verse Library, a journal with a calendar view, and the full King James Bible with every reference tappable.

See it in action

Good to know

  • Available for iPhone and iPad. There is no Android version.
  • Reflections and prayers are generated from Scripture and are not reviewed by clergy. The app is a companion for prayer, not a replacement for church, pastoral care, counseling, or emergency help.
  • Crisis resources (call or text 988 in the US) are linked on every reflection screen.
  • Be Still works offline and is free. Read how your data is handled in the Privacy Policy.
