From the Jesus Says app

Three quiet moments to rest in God’s presence.

Still Waters is one of the Be Still moments in Jesus Says: Daily Reflection. In the app you can also calm a storm-tossed sea in Peace, Be Still (Mark 4:39) and look up at a sky full of stars in Consider the Heavens (Psalm 147:4). Each ends with a verse. No goals, nothing to finish, and Be Still works offline.

When you are ready for more than a minute, the app’s daily card gives you one verse, one reflection, one prayer, and one step for today.
